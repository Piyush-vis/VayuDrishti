National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) Guidelines
Launched: January 2019 by Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)

1. Objectives and Goals:
   - Formulate a national framework for air quality management with a time-bound reduction target.
   - Goal: 20% to 30% reduction in PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations by 2024 (compared to 2017 baseline).
   - In 2022, the government revised the target to a 40% reduction in particulate matter concentrations by 2026.

2. Coverage:
   - Targets 131 non-attainment cities across India. These are cities that did not meet the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) consecutively for 5 years.
   - Includes cities under the Million-Plus Cities Challenge (funded by the 15th Finance Commission).

3. Key Components:
   - Augmentation of air quality monitoring networks (setting up manual and Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Systems - CAAQMS).
   - Establishing the National Knowledge Network (NKN) comprising academic institutions (IITs, NEERI) to provide technical assistance.
   - Preparation of city-specific clean air action plans addressing local sources: vehicular emissions, road dust, industrial pollution, waste burning, construction activity.
   - Development of emergency response protocols (similar to the Graded Response Action Plan - GRAP in Delhi-NCR).
   - Public awareness campaigns and multi-lingual health communication networks.
