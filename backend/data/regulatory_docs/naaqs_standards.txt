CPCB National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)
Notification Date: 18th November, 2009

The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) has set standard limits for ambient air quality parameters across India to protect public health and the environment.

1. Particulate Matter 2.5 (PM2.5):
   - Annual Average Limit: 40 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 60 µg/m³
   - Measured using Gravimetric / Beta Attenuation method.

2. Particulate Matter 10 (PM10):
   - Annual Average Limit: 60 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 100 µg/m³
   - Measured using Gravimetric / TEOM method.

3. Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2):
   - Annual Average Limit: 40 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 80 µg/m³
   - Measured using Jacob & Hochheiser / Chemiluminescence method.

4. Sulphur Dioxide (SO2):
   - Annual Average Limit: 50 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 80 µg/m³
   - Measured using Improved West & Gaeke / UV Fluorescence method.

5. Ozone (O3):
   - 8-Hour Average Limit: 100 µg/m³
   - 1-Hour Average Limit: 180 µg/m³
   - Measured using UV Photometric / Chemiluminescence method.

6. Carbon Monoxide (CO):
   - 8-Hour Average Limit: 2 mg/m³ (2000 µg/m³)
   - 1-Hour Average Limit: 4 mg/m³ (4000 µg/m³)
   - Measured using Non-Dispersive Infrared Spectroscopy (NDIR).

7. Ammonia (NH3):
   - Annual Average Limit: 100 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 400 µg/m³
   - Measured using Indophenol blue method.

8. Lead (Pb):
   - Annual Average Limit: 0.50 µg/m³
   - 24-Hour Average Limit: 1.0 µg/m³
   - Measured using AAS / ICP-MS after high-volume sampling.
