# VayuDrishti — Simple Demo Video Script (2.0 – 2.5 Minutes)

> A natural, screen-tour script designed for a quick 2-to-2.5 minute video walkthrough.
> Speak naturally in simple, conversational English.

---

### 🕒 Scene 1: Introduction & Command Center (0:00 – 0:30)
**[Screen: Command Center / Home Page — http://localhost:5173]**

* **What to do on screen:** Click a few station markers on the map, toggle the **Heatmap** and **Vulnerabilities** buttons, and drag the **Time Slider**.

* **Script / Voiceover:**
> "Hi everyone! This is **VayuDrishti**, our AI-powered urban air quality platform for smart cities.
> 
> Right now, we’re looking at the main **Command Center**. On the interactive map, you can see live monitoring stations across major Indian cities. Clicking any station gives us instant pollutant breakdowns, safe-limit comparisons, and 24-hour AQI trends.
> 
> We can also overlay thermal heatmaps, check vulnerable locations like schools and hospitals, and use the time slider to inspect historical trends."

---

### 🕒 Scene 2: Incident War Room (0:30 – 1:15)
**[Screen: Click "Incident War Room" on the left Sidebar]**

* **What to do on screen:** Click **Incident War Room**. Watch the 6-step timeline light up. Click the **Dispatch Voice Advisory** button so 3 seconds of Hindi audio plays.

* **Script / Voiceover:**
> "Next is our **Incident War Room**. This is where our automated intervention engine runs.
> 
> When a station detects rising pollution, the platform automatically runs a 6-step pipeline in seconds:
> 1. It picks up the high AQI **signal**.
> 2. Calculates **source attribution** like traffic vs crop fires.
> 3. Traces wind **back-trajectories** across active satellite fire hotspots.
> 4. Auto-drafts official **GRAP emergency action orders** before pollution peaks.
> 5. Calculates **human impact** like exposed population and health risks.
> 6. And sends out **voice advisories** in regional Indian languages.
> 
> All of this happens in seconds, compared to manual government responses that usually take over 24 hours."

---

### 🕒 Scene 3: 72-Hour Predictions (1:15 – 1:40)
**[Screen: Click "72H Predictions" on the Sidebar]**

* **What to do on screen:** Show the 72-hour forecast line chart and hover over the confidence interval band. Point at the Active Forecast Alerts list.

* **Script / Voiceover:**
> "Moving over to **72-Hour Predictions**.
> 
> Here, our machine learning models forecast AQI levels for the next 3 days, complete with confidence interval bands. It highlights upcoming threshold breaches early so city administrators can take action before the air gets dangerous."

---

### 🕒 Scene 4: Enforcement & Citizen Advisory (1:40 – 2:10)
**[Screen: Click "Enforcement Desk", then click "Citizen Portal"]**

* **What to do on screen:** In Enforcement, show the priority-ranked action list. In Citizen Portal, switch languages (e.g. Hindi, Tamil, Marathi).

* **Script / Voiceover:**
> "On the **Enforcement Desk**, field teams get prioritized action tasks — like deploying dust suppression at construction sites or rerouting heavy traffic.
> 
> And on the **Citizen Portal**, residents get personalized health advice in 6 different Indian languages — making critical safety warnings accessible to everyone."

---

### 🕒 Scene 5: Multi-City Analytics & AI Assistant (2:10 – 2:30)
**[Screen: Click "City Analytics", then click the floating Bot icon]**

* **What to do on screen:** Show the multi-city comparison chart and the live CPCB data feed table. Finally, click the bottom-right **Bot icon** to pop open the chat window.

* **Script / Voiceover:**
> "Finally, **City Analytics** lets us compare air quality averages across Indian metros using live CPCB government data feeds covering over 500 stations nationwide.
> 
> We also have a built-in **AI Compliance Assistant** at the bottom right, which answers questions about official CPCB rules and air quality standards.
> 
> That’s a quick overview of VayuDrishti. Thanks for watching!"
